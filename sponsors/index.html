🌟 Headline Sponsor

AJ Trade Plastics

AJ Trade Plastics was established in 2012 to change people's perception of the double glazing industry. Since then they have grown to offer an even wider range of products including cladding, wall panels, flooring and more. They pride themselves on their customer service and doing the right thing by their customers, offering top brands at everyday low prices. AJ's mission is to make home improvement affordable to all.

As a local family owned, and run, company they understand what is important to their customers. Quality, service and value. If it is important to you, it is important to AJ's.

AJ Trade Plastics believe in supporting our community and are very proud supporters of many local good causes and charitable events.
